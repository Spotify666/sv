01 / The House

Built on the line, not the trend

Every piece begins as a silhouette, a measured outline before it is ever a colour or a fabric. The house is known for how that line evolves: western construction carrying Indian surface craft, drawn for the way women actually move.

Sashi Vangapalli opened Mugdha Art Studio in 2012 and built the couture label alongside it, dressing brides, film and stage through a decade of work in Hyderabad, including the Cannes Film Festival red carpet in 2017 and the runway at India Runway Fashion Week.

The couture line is cut for occasion: bridal and reception, lehengas, sarees, gowns and indo-western, with a menswear practice running beside it. Pret sits underneath, made by the same hand in fewer hours.

03 / Commission

How a piece is made here

01

Consultation

An appointment at the Banjara Hills atelier. Occasion, palette and the shape you want to be in.

02

The sketch

The silhouette is drawn and agreed before anything is cut. Nothing proceeds on assumption.

03

Fabric & craft

Cloth, embroidery and surface work are selected together, because on this line they decide each other.

04

Fittings

Successive in-atelier fittings against the drawing, adjusting the line rather than the size.

05

Finish

Final press, fastenings and handover, with the piece checked on you and not on a stand.
